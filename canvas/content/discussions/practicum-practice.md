## Practice Practicum

Use this discussion to complete and compare approaches to the practice
practicum.

1. [Open the Practice Practicum project in Posit Cloud]({{ practicum_practice_url }}).
2. Read the prompt and guidelines before class.
3. Complete the work independently under the stated practice conditions.
4. Post your rendered PDF and a brief note identifying the question you chose.
5. After submitting, review at least one classmate's approach. Focus on the
   analytical choice, not whether the classmate reached the same conclusion.

Do not post source data, personal information, or material from the actual final
practicum. Follow the AI and collaboration rules stated in the practice prompt.
