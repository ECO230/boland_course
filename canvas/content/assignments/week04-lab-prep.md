## Purpose

Bring a short example of your own quantitative writing to class so you can
practice revising it for clarity, audience, and action. You do not need to write
a new paragraph for this preparation task.

## Complete the preparation

1. Find one paragraph from this semester or a previous course in which you
   discussed data, statistics, or a quantitative result.
2. Remove names, confidential information, or restricted data that should not
   be shared with classmates.
3. Submit the paragraph in Canvas by either pasting it into the text-entry box
   or uploading a `.docx` or `.pdf` file.
4. Keep an editable copy available during class in OneDrive, on your computer,
   or in another location you can access reliably.

## Submission

Submit one existing paragraph in the Lab Prep for Lab 4 Canvas assignment
before class. This is a 10-point pass/fail preparation task. A complete
submission contains a quantitative claim or interpretation that can be revised
during the lab.

## Checklist

- [ ] My paragraph discusses data or statistics.
- [ ] I removed confidential or identifying information.
- [ ] I submitted the paragraph in Canvas.
- [ ] I can edit a copy during class.

## Grading focus

Full credit requires a usable writing sample submitted before class. The
quality of the original paragraph is not graded; the purpose is to provide
material for revision.
