## Clean Dataset

Prepare the shared dataset in R using `team/prepare_data.qmd`, explaining your filters, missing-value decisions, derived variables, and any joins. You are not expected to complete all cleaning, joining, or coding tasks on your own: the instructor can help wherever needed. Outline your sources and data needs in your current `team/analysis_plan.qmd`; changes since the checkpoint submission are fine because it is a working document.

**Submit `team/prepare_data.pdf` for your team. Do not upload the clean CSV.** Keep the named CSV and editable preparation source in the group's Posit Cloud project. Include your group number, teammate names, project link, and exact clean CSV filename in the PDF or submission comment.
