## Clean Dataset: Progress Checkpoint

The instructor is handling the bulk of the cleaning. Your team's task this week
is to check that the prepared data fits your project and start using it.

1. **Download and open your clean data.** In your group Posit Cloud project's
   Files pane, check the clean `.csv` file, then choose **More > Export**.
   Check that the observations and variables support your current questions.
2. **Try one analysis.** Begin a table or chart in Excel or Tableau, or attempt
   a hypothesis test in R. Save your work. Describe an initial result or the
   specific problem that stopped you. If the file is unavailable, explain which
   analysis you are ready to start and what data you need.
3. **Submit a quick team reflection.** One teammate submits a text entry or short
   PDF for the team. Include your group number and exact clean CSV filename(s).
   You may also identify a saved analysis file or its location (optional).

Use four brief bullets:

- **Data access:** What file did you download and open, or what blocked access?
- **Fit for your needs:** Which question does it support, which variables did
  you check, and what limitations did you find?
- **Analysis progress:** What tool and variables did you use, what did you try,
  and what did you find or where did you get stuck?
- **Requests and next step:** What specific changes or new data do you need,
  and why? If none, say so. What will your team do next, and who will do it?

A few sentences per bullet is enough. A screenshot or output excerpt is optional.
**Do not upload the clean CSV.** Keep your data and analysis files in your group
project or shared team workspace. A preparation PDF is not required for this
checkpoint.

This is a **pass/fail team progress checkpoint** under the existing prep-work
policy. A complete, specific reflection can receive credit even if your analysis
is blocked; explain the blocker and what you need to move forward.
