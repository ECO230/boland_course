## Analysis Plan

Complete your team's plan in **`team/analysis_plan.qmd`** in your group's Posit Cloud project. All main project documents and rendered PDFs are in the **`team/` directory**.

1. Open `team/analysis_plan.qmd` and complete the team's stories, hypotheses, variables, planned analyses, cleaning decisions, and responsibilities.
2. Select **Render** to create **`team/analysis_plan.pdf`** beside the source document.
3. Submit the rendered plan PDF using this Canvas assignment, or use the text entry to identify the project link and the plan's location. Include your group number and teammate names so the instructor can find your team's work.

Keep the editable `team/analysis_plan.qmd` in the project and continue updating it after this checkpoint as your questions and methods develop.
