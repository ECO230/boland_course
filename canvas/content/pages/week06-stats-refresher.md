## Statistics Refresher

Read these 3 articles in order. Together they explain why sample estimates need
an uncertainty check, how alpha and the p-value are used, and how confidence
intervals describe the precision of an estimate.

1. [Why We Need to Use Hypothesis Tests](https://blog.minitab.com/en/adventures-in-statistics-2/understanding-hypothesis-tests-why-we-need-to-use-hypothesis-tests-in-statistics)
2. [Significance Levels (Alpha) and P-values](https://blog.minitab.com/en/blog/adventures-in-statistics-2/understanding-hypothesis-tests-significance-levels-alpha-and-p-values-in-statistics)
3. [Confidence Intervals and Confidence Levels](https://blog.minitab.com/en/blog/adventures-in-statistics-2/understanding-hypothesis-tests-confidence-intervals-and-confidence-levels)

As you read, be prepared to explain:

- why a difference in a sample does not automatically establish a difference
  in the population;
- what a p-value does and does not measure;
- why the alpha level should be selected before seeing the result; and
- how the width of a confidence interval communicates precision.
