## Pew Research Center Methods 101: Sampling

Pew Research Center conducts public-opinion survey research and publishes
methodological explanations of how those surveys are designed. Watch these 2
short Methods 101 videos before class:

1. [How can a survey of 1,000 people tell you what the whole U.S. thinks?](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2017/05/12/methods-101-random-sampling/)
   (about 2.5 minutes)
2. [What are nonprobability surveys?](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2018/08/06/what-are-nonprobability-surveys/)
   (about 4.5 minutes)

As you watch, be prepared to explain how random selection differs from simply
collecting a large number of responses and why an opt-in survey may not
represent its intended population.
