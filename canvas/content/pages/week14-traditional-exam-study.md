## How Should I Study for the Traditional Exam Portion?

### Finish and revisit the course material

Return to readings, guided notes, activities, or practice work that you skipped
or completed too quickly. Focus on explaining why an analytical method fits a
question rather than memorizing isolated definitions.

### Review your past quizzes

Use the review windows configured in Canvas to revisit earlier practice and
graded quizzes. For every missed question, explain why the correct answer fits
and why your original answer did not.

### Organize your guided notes

Review the completed guided notes for data literacy, descriptive statistics,
visualization, communication, hypothesis testing, inferential methods, survey
research, machine learning, and experimental design.

You will retain the material better if you reorganize important ideas in your
own words. Build a short handwritten reference that connects:

- the analytical question;
- variable types and study design;
- the appropriate table, graph, or statistical method;
- the evidence needed for interpretation; and
- the limitations that affect the conclusion.

### Practice making decisions

For each major course method, practice answering these questions without
looking at a model answer:

1. What does one row represent?
2. What question is the analysis answering?
3. Which method fits the variables and design?
4. What result would change the business decision?
5. What claim would go beyond the available evidence?
