## What Will the Final Be Like?

The final has 2 parts:

1. **Traditional exam:** A cumulative assessment similar in format to the
   practice and graded quizzes used throughout the course.
2. **Practicum:** A short applied analysis in which you receive a prompt and a
   dataset and demonstrate the course's core data-analysis skills.

You will have 120 minutes to complete both parts. Complete the final during the
scheduled exam period: **{{ final_exam_schedule }}**.

::: {.callout-note appearance="minimal" icon="false" title="Technical problems"}
If you encounter a technical problem during the final, contact your instructor
promptly and describe the issue. Do not panic or begin a second attempt unless
your instructor directs you to do so.
:::

The current practicum prompt, permitted resources, required software, and
submission requirements will be published separately in Canvas.
