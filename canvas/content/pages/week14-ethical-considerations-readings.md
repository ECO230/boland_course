## Ethical Considerations: Additional Readings

Read these 4 articles about how data and algorithms can affect education,
employment, credit, and public health:

1. [Under a Watchful Eye: How Colleges Are Tracking Students to Boost Graduation](https://www.apmreports.org/episode/2019/08/06/college-data-tracking-students-graduation)
2. [Four Unethical Uses of AI in Recruitment](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tomaspremuzic/2018/05/27/four-unethical-uses-of-ai-in-recruitment/)
3. [Credit Denial in the Age of AI](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/credit-denial-in-the-age-of-ai/)
4. [Digital Contact Tracing Will Fail Unless Privacy Is Respected, Experts Warn](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2020/apr/20/coronavirus-digital-contact-tracing-will-fail-unless-privacy-is-respected-experts-warn)

For each reading, identify:

- the decision being made;
- the people affected by the decision;
- the data or proxy measures being used;
- a potential benefit;
- a potential harm or inequity; and
- one form of oversight, transparency, consent, or appeal that could reduce
  the risk.
