Great Lakes Synthetic Health Records

Longitudinal records for simulated Wisconsin and Minnesota patients

A fully synthetic relational health-record dataset for operational and analytical practice
Warning

Every patient and event is synthetic. Results describe a simulation, not real people, disease prevalence, treatment effectiveness, clinical quality, or population health.

Note

Coverage: Simulated histories through December 31, 2025
Geography: Wisconsin and Minnesota
Patients: 5,694
License: Synthea software, Apache 2.0; adapted release, CC BY 4.0

About this dataset

This release resembles a vendor-neutral relational electronic health-record warehouse. It supports operational questions about appointments and no-shows, patient-satisfaction response, utilization, encounters, costs, payers, service mix, and longitudinal care. It is not a copy of Epic Clarity or Caboodle.

The appointment and satisfaction tables are ECO 230 teaching extensions. They intentionally contain plausible relationships and data-quality issues students can investigate, but those relationships are not estimates from a real health system.

Core and analysis files

Course assignments provide .csv extracts from these tables. Extracts may cover a smaller time period, state, or type of care than the complete dataset.

How the files relate

  • patient_key connects a fictional patient across time.
  • encounter_key connects clinical facts, appointments that became encounters, surveys, and claims to a visit.
  • organization_key, provider_key, and payer_key connect encounters to facilities, clinicians, and insurers.
  • claim_key connects claims to claim transactions.
  • county_fips supports a join from patients to compatible county-level population or geographic reference data.

One encounter may have many diagnoses, procedures, medications, observations, and claim transactions. Aggregate each fact table independently before joining it to encounters.

Important limitations and designed issues

  • A blank appointment encounter_key does not automatically mean no-show; use appointment_status to distinguish no-shows, cancellations, and rescheduling.
  • Satisfaction includes invited nonrespondents. Ratings exist only when response_status == "responded", so response bias can be analyzed.
  • Encounter facts occur at different times. Results recorded after an encounter begins may leak future information into a prediction model.
  • Follow-up is censored at the simulation end date.
  • A small number of source encounters occur after a recorded synthetic death date. They are intentionally preserved as a discoverable quality issue.
  • Costs, coverage, outcomes, and utilization are simulated.

Terminology, privacy, and provenance

The records were generated with Synthea 4.0.0. No record describes a real person, and names, addresses, phone numbers, exact coordinates, source UUIDs, device identifiers, and free-text notes are removed.

Diagnosis and procedure records use broad, independently authored two-level ECO 230 teaching groups. Individual ICD-10-CM, CPT, HCPCS, CDT, SNOMED CT, and private crosswalk content is not released. The private build retains auditable source-to-group mappings; those mappings are not part of the student dataset.
