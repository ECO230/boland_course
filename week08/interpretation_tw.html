Steps in Hypothesis Testing

  1. What statistical story am I attempting to tell?

What are we trying to find out?

  1. What have I estimated or plotted… If no uncertainty, what should the business do?

What do we expect to happen?

  1. Which test is appropriate and what is the null hypothesis?

How are we going to test it?

  1. What is the statistical interpretation of the test result?

  1. What is the practical interpretation of this analysis for a business audience?

Does it matter?

t-Tests

Comparing Means between Two Categories

Independent Samples t-Test idt()


Steps 1-3: Setup


Test: Independent Samples t-test
Function: idt()


H0: \(\mu\)Black = \(\mu\)Green
HA: \(\mu\)Black \(\neq\) \(\mu\)Green
α = 0.05


Run the Test

h3 <- df %>%
  idt(scones ~ tea)


Step 4: Do we believe it?

h3$results

t(21.39) = -1.681, p = 0.107, d = -0.686, bf10 = 1.013


Decision Rule:

  • If p \(\leq \alpha\) → Reject H0
  • If p > \(\alpha\) → Fail to Reject H0


Decision:
p = 0.107 > 0.05 → Fail to Reject H0
