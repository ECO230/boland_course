Guided Notes 2: Descriptive Statistics and Data Literacy

NotePurpose

Use this guide with the Week 2 readings and class activities. Your completed notes should help you select and interpret descriptive statistics based on the question, variable type, and distribution.

Objectives

  • choose appropriate measures of center and spread;
  • identify the denominator used in a percentage;
  • distinguish frequency, cross-tabulation, and grouped-summary tables;
  • compare measures of association; and
  • explain why a summary is or is not meaningful for a variable.
