Inside Airbnb source data
-------------------------

The source snapshots are published by Inside Airbnb:

https://insideairbnb.com/get-the-data/
https://insideairbnb.com/data-policies/

Inside Airbnb states that its data is licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Inside Airbnb's Data Policies page also instructs users not to republish the
data. Because those statements create an unresolved redistribution condition,
this transformed release is a local evaluation candidate only. It must not be
uploaded to data.60land.com or distributed to students until written approval
or another documented resolution is obtained.

Course transformation
---------------------

ECO 230 combines the June 2026 Chicago and Twin Cities MSA snapshots; selects
structured analytical fields; replaces source listing identifiers with
release-local keys; excludes host and reviewer identity fields and free text;
aggregates reviews to listing-month counts; derives snapshot-price benchmarks,
listed-availability and stay-restriction summaries; and adds documented
geographic keys. The source calendars do not contain daily prices.

The source's approximate coordinates are retained. Inside Airbnb says listing
locations are anonymized by displacement of up to 150 metres from the actual
address. No warranty is made for source completeness, positional accuracy, or
the course transformations.
