Adapted release
---------------

The ECO 230 release is an adaptation that selects, renames, types, aggregates,
and privacy-reduces source fields. It is distributed for noncommercial course
use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Users must credit Olist and ECO 230, must not use the
data for commercial purposes, and must distribute adaptations under the same
license.

The release excludes source identifiers, review text, exact coordinates,
customer and seller addresses, and sales-representative identifiers. Stable
release-local pseudonymous keys are provided only to support relational joins.

No warranty is made regarding completeness, accuracy, fitness for a particular
purpose, or continued availability of the upstream Kaggle downloads.
