Chicago Traffic course release

City of Chicago source data
---------------------------

Crash, vehicle, person, and ZIP-region records are provided by the City of
Chicago through the Chicago Data Portal:

https://data.cityofchicago.org/d/85ca-t3if
https://data.cityofchicago.org/d/68nd-jvt3
https://data.cityofchicago.org/d/u6pd-qa9d
https://data.cityofchicago.org/d/rpca-8um6

Use is subject to the City of Chicago Data Portal Terms of Use. The City makes
the data available as-is and does not warrant its accuracy, timeliness, or
completeness. This course release identifies the source vintage and describes
its modifications in metadata.json, source_manifest.json, and
DATA-DICTIONARY.md.

City data terms and disclaimer:
https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/narr/foia/data_disclaimer.html

NOAA numerical weather
----------------------

Numerical weather observations come from NOAA National Centers for
Environmental Information Local Climatological Data for Chicago O'Hare
International Airport (station 72530094846) and Chicago Midway Airport
(station 72534014819).

https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/local-climatological-data
https://doi.org/10.25921/96dw-mb77

Suggested citation:

National Centers for Environmental Information. Local Climatological Data,
subset for Chicago O'Hare and Chicago Midway, 2018-2025. Access date and exact
source-file checksums are recorded in source_manifest.json.

Course transformation
---------------------

ECO 230 selects complete calendar years 2018-2025; applies stable names and
types; removes selected location and administrative identifiers from the
person and vehicle tables; maps the portal's ZIP-region identifier to ZIP5;
and attaches the nearest qualifying NOAA observation to the one-row-per-crash
analysis table. No warranty is made for the adapted files.
