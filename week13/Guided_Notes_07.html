Guided Notes 7: Machine Learning and Experimental Research

NotePurpose

Use this guide with the Week 13 readings, videos, demonstrations, and lecture. It connects advanced analytics and machine-learning concepts to the design choices that make causal evidence more or less credible.

Keep the completed guide as a reference for Project 2, quizzes, and the final.

Objectives

  • distinguish descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics;
  • explain how training data, test data, features, and labels are used;
  • compare classification, clustering, and regression tasks;
  • explain generalization, overfitting, and model validation;
  • identify treatments, outcomes, controls, confounds, and demand characteristics;
  • compare common experimental and quasi-experimental designs; and
  • distinguish internal validity from external validity.
