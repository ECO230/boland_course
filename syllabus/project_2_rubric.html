Project 2: Rubric

Points and Deliverables

Deliverable Points
Week 13 informal topic meeting 10
Week 14 research proposal pitch 10
Week 14 Red Team critique exercise 10
Written Research Proposal 70
Total 100

The Written Research Proposal is one 2-4 page paper in paragraph form. The four written criteria below score different aspects of that same paper. They are not four submissions.

This is a study-design and research-proposal exercise. Students do not carry out the research, collect data, or perform analysis. Evaluate the proposed design and the reasons for its choices. No completed results are required.

See the Project 2 prompt for the required components and the Research Proposal Outline for planning questions.

Week 13 Informal Topic Meeting (10 Points)

In-class topic discussion, worth 10 points.

Professional Quality (10 points)

Team brings a proposed topic, explains the problem or opportunity and decision maker, and describes a focused question and a workable direction for the proposed study.

Meets Expectations (8 points)

Team brings a workable topic, but the problem, decision maker, question, or proposed approach needs clarification.

Needs Revision (5 points)

Team has a topic but cannot yet explain the problem, question, or how it could be studied.

Missing (0 points)

Team does not participate or does not bring a proposed topic.

Week 14 Research Proposal Pitch (10 Points)

In-class pitch to a small set of teams, worth 10 points.

Professional Quality (10 points)

Pitch explains the problem, question, proposed study design, reasons for the design choices, and main risks. Team responds to questions about the proposed work.

Meets Expectations (8 points)

Pitch explains the proposal, but a design choice, its rationale, or an important risk remains unclear.

Needs Revision (5 points)

Pitch omits major parts of the proposed study or is difficult to follow.

Missing (0 points)

Team does not present a research proposal.

Week 14 Red Team Critique Exercise (10 Points)

In-class critique exercise, worth 10 points within Project 2.

Professional Quality (10 points)

Team identifies specific sampling, measurement, design, ethical, or interpretation concerns in another proposal and discusses changes that could improve it.

Meets Expectations (8 points)

Team identifies relevant concerns, but the explanation or suggested changes need more detail.

Needs Revision (5 points)

Feedback is too general or incomplete to help the presenting team revise its proposal.

Missing (0 points)

Team does not participate in the critique exercise.

Written Proposal - Problem or Opportunity (15 Points)

One criterion within the same 70-point Written Research Proposal.

Professional Quality (15 points)

Paper identifies the problem or opportunity, explains why it matters, and names the decision maker and the decision the proposed study would inform.

Meets Expectations (12 points)

Paper identifies the problem or opportunity but needs a clearer explanation of its importance, decision maker, or intended use.

Needs Revision (8 points)

Problem or opportunity is vague, and the paper does not explain how the proposed study would inform a decision.

Missing (0 points)

Problem or opportunity is missing.

Written Proposal - Research Question and Direction (15 Points)

One criterion within the same 70-point Written Research Proposal.

Professional Quality (15 points)

Paper states a focused research question, explains its scope, and connects it to the decision. Any objectives or tentative hypotheses clarify the proposed work.

Meets Expectations (12 points)

Question is workable but needs a clearer scope or connection to the decision and study design.

Needs Revision (8 points)

Question is unclear, too broad, or poorly connected to the proposed study.

Missing (0 points)

Research question is missing.

Written Proposal - Study Design and Measures (20 Points)

One criterion within the same 70-point Written Research Proposal. Evaluate the proposed work, not completed research.

Professional Quality (20 points)

Paper explains and justifies the proposed method, population, sample selection, main concepts and measures, and data collection process. The reader can understand how the study would be conducted.

Meets Expectations (16 points)

Design fits the question, but the method, sampling, measures, or planned collection process needs more detail.

Needs Revision (10 points)

Design is incomplete or does not explain how the proposed method and measures would answer the question.

Missing (0 points)

Proposed study design and measures are missing.

Written Proposal - Sampling, Measurement, Conclusions, Risks, and Ethics (20 Points)

One criterion within the same 70-point Written Research Proposal. No data collection, study implementation, or analysis is required.

Professional Quality (20 points)

Paper explains representation, bias, measurement concerns, and limits on generalization or causal claims. It identifies specific risks and ethical concerns, explains how they would be addressed, and acknowledges remaining limitations.

Meets Expectations (16 points)

Paper addresses the quality of the proposed evidence, risks, and ethics, but overlooks an important concern or needs to explain its effect on conclusions.

Needs Revision (10 points)

Discussion gives general cautions with little connection to this study or omits major sampling, interpretation, or ethical concerns.

Missing (0 points)

Discussion of evidence quality, risks, limitations, and ethics is missing.
