Guided Notes 1: Data Literacy

NotePurpose

Use this guide while completing the Week 1 readings and participating in class. Your notes should help you recognize common data-structure mistakes before you begin calculating statistics.

Objectives

  • identify the unit of analysis and grain;
  • distinguish observations, variables, values, and identifiers;
  • evaluate whether a table follows tidy-data principles;
  • distinguish dimensions from measures; and
  • classify variables using appropriate measurement scales.
